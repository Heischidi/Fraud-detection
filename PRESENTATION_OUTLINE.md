# 7-minute presentation plan (6–7 slides)

1. **Title and research question** — 30 seconds.
2. **Why fraud detection is difficult** — class imbalance and the cost of missed fraud / false alarms; 50 seconds.
3. **Dataset and preparation** — source, target, prevalence, features, duplicate handling; 60 seconds.
4. **Models and evaluation** — Logistic Regression, Random Forest, class weighting, PR-AUC/precision/recall/F1; 70 seconds.
5. **Methodological experiment** — random versus chronological split, hypothesis and design; 80 seconds.
6. **Results and interpretation** — actual charts and metrics; 100 seconds.
7. **Limitations, trade-offs and conclusion** — 50 seconds.

Keep slides visual. Speak in your own words; be prepared to justify every modelling and preprocessing decision.
